# Errata Summary

Status: initial planning summary, not a complete rules extract.

The February 2024 errata overrides the base rulebook where it applies. Each affected feature phase must re-check the exact errata wording before implementation.

## Movement And ZOC

- Quarter-turn and half-turn restrictions include an errata clarification: do not combine multiple quarter/half turns in the same movement except where light troop rules allow it.
- Light troops have corrected wording for a free first half-turn or quarter-turn at the beginning or end of a move.
- ZOC movement includes clarified permissions for charging the most-threatening enemy and aligning before that charge.
- Quarter-turn or half-turn in place can be allowed to face an enemy exerting ZOC on rear or flank even if part of the front edge leaves that ZOC.
- ZOC is not exerted into, from, or while in terrain that penalizes the unit in combat.
- Some interpenetration permissions are front-to-rear or rear-to-front only and do not allow lateral sliding except for specified units.

## Charge, Evade, Contact, And Conformation

- Charge range, group charge, and minimum advance clarifications affect who may conform and how turn movement counts.
- Evading units move straight up to the adjusted evade distance, with group split exceptions where required to avoid obstacles or units.
- Flank and rear contact restrictions apply to charges and moves to provide melee support, not to simple support.
- Conformation can use rear-corner alignment in specified physically blocked flank-contact cases, but not as a way to avoid ZOC.
- ZOC priority can prevent required conformation to another enemy already in contact.
- Some units cannot be shifted to allow conformation, including war wagons, heavy artillery, and units defending specific obstacles or stakes.

## Combat And Results

- Several troop factors and abilities are corrected or clarified, including cavalry, knights, furious charge, armour, impact, polearm, war wagons with blades, light infantry destruction, flank/rear attacks, and pursuit.
- Commander engaged-in-combat status is narrowed: a commander in melee support is not the same as a commander fighting with the main melee unit.
- Terrain and fortification corrections affect shooting, rivers, bridges, hills, roads, ambushes, and deployment.

## Army Lists

- Multiple printed army-list corrections affect dates, allies, points, unit availability, and options.
- Army list JSON must support errata overlays instead of editing base data silently.
